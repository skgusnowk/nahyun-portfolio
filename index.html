06 / ABOUT THE PRACTICENAHYUN KWON

I DON'T BEGIN
WITH A medium.
I BEGIN WITH
A question.

My background moves across design, interactive media, visual art and research, but I do not see those disciplines as the boundaries of my practice. Each is a different way to ask questions, shape meaning and bring new experiences into the world.

I want to build creative, human-centered work that reaches beyond familiar formats: interactive environments, experimental interfaces, accessible experiences and new forms of storytelling. I am drawn to what happens when technology, critical inquiry and artistic imagination meet—and to designing for people, not simply for platforms.

Living across South Korea, Singapore, Saudi Arabia, Germany and now the United States has taught me to move between languages, cultures and ways of seeing. That experience shapes the questions I bring to design: who is this for, what assumptions are embedded in it, and what becomes possible when people encounter an unfamiliar perspective?
